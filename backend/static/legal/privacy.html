Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Welcome to CircleUp. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe experience in your neighborhood sharing community.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture (via Google Auth or direct sign-up).
  • Location Data: Your precise location is used only to show you nearby tools on the Radar screen and calculate neighborhood proximity.
  • Media: Photos of tools you list for lending (stored securely on AWS S3).
  • Activity data: Borrowing history, Karma points, and community ratings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to provide the CircleUp service, facilitate tool sharing between neighbors, verify trustworthiness via the Karma system, and improve the app's features.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and managed database services. We use AWS S3 for media storage and PostgreSQL for user/tool records.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. Your name and approximate location (within the society radius) are visible to other verified members of your neighborhood when you list or borrow a tool.

5. Your Rights

You can request to delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting our support team.
